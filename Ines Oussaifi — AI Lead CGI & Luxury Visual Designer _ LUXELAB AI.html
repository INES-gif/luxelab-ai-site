Ines Oussaifi

AI Lead CGI & Luxury Visual Designer | LUXELAB AI

Ines Oussaifi stands at the vanguard of AI-driven CGI design and virtual architecture. Her studio, LUXELAB AI, bridges European heritage craftsmanship with generative technology to redefine luxury brand storytelling.

"Quiet luxury is defined not by ornament, but by proportion, light, and understated visual weight."

Her work spans digital flagship store architecture, haute couture textile physics, and spatial set design for fashion shows—helping global legacy brands translate their physical heritage into hyper-realistic virtual spaces.
